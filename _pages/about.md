**Research Interests** : Deep Learning, Optimal Transport, Stable Matching, Graph Learning

I am a final-year PhD student at the Australian National University. My PhD research spans both the theoretical foundations and practical applications of modern graph matching, exploring how concepts from stable matching and optimal transport theory can be leveraged to develop novel learning frameworks for graph matching.

Prior to starting my PhD, I worked as a Research and Development Engineer, delivering solutions in high-performance domain drop catching, factory scheduling optimization, and edge computing. I completed my BSc (Hons) degree in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka. 

I am an avid cricket fan and enjoy 80s music. In my leisure time, I enjoy swimming and hanging out with friends.
