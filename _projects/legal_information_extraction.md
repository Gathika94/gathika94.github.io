## Legal Information Extraction
 

Developing natural language processing based techniques to extract useful information from legal opinion texts. The project covered different areas of legal information extraction including legal party identification, legal sentiment analysis, legal argument extraction, and identifying relationships between sentences. One of the key challenge in developing legal information extraction systems is the domain specific terminology in the legal domain. We addressed these issues by applying domain adaptation techniques such as transfer learning.
